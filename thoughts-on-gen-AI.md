# Thoughts on Generative AI
To summarize everything I'm about to say in one sentence, generative AI is a great, but people aren't.

## Jargon
Whenever I'm referring to generative AI, I'm just going say "AI," but these terms should not be confused.

AI (Artificial Intelligence) is exactly as it sounds. It's the capacity of computers to perform things that typically require "human" intelligence.

Generative AI is a specific type of AI that can create "high-quality" content based on data it's been trained on; basically, the term refers to learning models with ungodly amounts of depth.

## Reality
Everything feels slightly off these days.

The majority of posters and billboards I walk by are either *about* AI or *use* AI. It literally does not matter where I'm walking, only that humans with access to technology are present there. Every time I enter the hospital I'm currently volunteering at, I just see a massive banner that screams AI art—generic, weird shadows, etc.

However, it's more often than not that AI usage in art these days is almost undetectable. You really have to evaluate something for mistakes that only a computer would make, and even then you aren't guaranteed to find a mistake.

## AI Usage
Honestly, when humans are still doing most of the creative and cognitive work, AI usage isn't terrible.

It's an efficient use of modern technology, in my opinion, when you understand how to do something but save time on it by using AI. That extra time can go towards doing the things you're actually interested in and want to do, and contributing those things to yourself and/or society usually ends up being for the better.

That's a lukewarm take at best, though. Almost every other opinion I've perceived is so polarized, which hurts my heart. Strong opinions are great, but if you still hope for change, you at least have to change (or be willing to) too. Most people forget that part when their opinions start possessing conviction.

It's fair to be passionate about this though. Efficient, human-oriented AI usage is such a fine line to walk that it practically a tightrope—maybe worse, like a raycast.

## Hope
I do believe that humanity has the power to overcome its problems, AI included.

Is there a chance we can fail and transition into a Wall-E scenario? Yeah, definitely. I'm terrified of that outcome, and I hope it doesn't come to fruition for the sake of my future kids.

Reading that last bit back to myself, almost everything positive in my headspace ran from me. The race isn't lost yet, though; if we make the right judgments moving forward, greatness can be achieved with or without AI—we need to be the ones in control.

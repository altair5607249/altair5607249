Hart is a sentient machine accidentally created by Altair, a university student, in a freak lab accident.

Instead of cashing in on his creation, Altair gives him a fake identity and a strict set of directives to live amongst humans.

As Hart breaks each directive, he learns their true purpose: to protect him from humanity. However, this doesn’t stop him from finding one true human friend, Mirai (望愛).

It is thanks to her kindness that he unravels an answer to the very question responsible for his existence.
